## 1. Purpose

This file defines **The White Stag** — the Destiny Herald — within the Aureon / Quantara architecture.

This archetype is fundamentally different from the Horses, Hawks, Ganesh, Valcor, or Shiva.

Where they deal with:

- **motion** (Horses)  
- **perception** (Hawks)  
- **gates** (Ganesh)  
- **probability** (Valcor)  
- **transformation** (Shiva)  

the **White Stag** deals with something deeper and more primordial:

> **Destiny, Calling, Turning Points, and Soul-Direction.**

The Stag does not respond to intention.  
He **initiates** intention.

The Stag appears **only** when a major evolutionary pivot is ready.
